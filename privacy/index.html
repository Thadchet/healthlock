Health Lock — Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Summary

Health Lock does not collect any of your data. Your health data stays on your iPhone: the app reads it from Apple Health, draws it into a wallpaper image on the device, and never sends it anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

What the app reads and why

The app only reads. It never writes to Apple Health. You choose what to allow in the Apple Health permission sheet and can change it at any time in the Health app.

The app sees only the single photo you pick; it has no access to the rest of your library. Goals you type in (weekly distance, race date, journey start) are kept as settings.

Where your data is stored

Everything stays on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage:

  • A summary of the health numbers the wallpaper needs (up to about 62 days), so the wallpaper can be drawn while the phone is locked
  • Your settings and the photo you picked (only one photo is kept)
  • A local log of recent wallpaper updates (time and result, no health values)

These files use iOS data protection. The app does not sync them anywhere; like other app data, they are part of your own iPhone backup if you have backups turned on. Your health data is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling, renting or sharing of any data
  • No use of health data for marketing or advertising

Purchases

Health Lock Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment and your Apple Account; we never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is active on this device. Apple's own privacy policy covers those transactions.

Shortcuts and your Lock Screen

The wallpaper is made by the app's "Get Health Wallpaper" action and set by a shortcut on your iPhone. The image stays on the device. Because it is your Lock Screen, anyone who sees your phone can see what it shows: use Hide numbers or Progress only in the app to keep values private.

Your choices

  • Turn off any health type in the Health app: Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Health Lock.
  • Delete all app data by deleting the app. Nothing remains with us because we never had it.

Children

Health Lock is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
