Health Lock — Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: b.bosskungzz@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days.

The wallpaper doesn't update

  • Open the Shortcuts app and check that the shortcut Health Lock Wallpaper exists. If not, add it again from Health Lock › Settings › Set up the shortcut again.
  • Turn on its triggers (Charger is connected, Workout ends, Time of day) and set each to Run Immediately.
  • In Health Lock › Settings › Run log you can see every update and why one failed.

Numbers are missing or show 0

  • Check what the app may read: Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Health Lock.
  • Heart and readiness looks need an Apple Watch. Tell the app in Settings › Apple Health.
  • iPhone locks Apple Health while the phone is locked, so the wallpaper uses the last numbers it read and shows when they were updated.

The wallpaper looks zoomed in

Open the shortcut, tap the Set Wallpaper action and turn off Show Preview and Crop to Subject.

Pro and purchases

  • Bought Pro on another device? Health Lock › Settings › Restore purchases.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
